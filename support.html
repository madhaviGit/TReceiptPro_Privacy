Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete a receipt or transaction?

Swipe left on any receipt or transaction in your list, or open it and tap the delete icon. Deleted items go to Trash, where you can restore them or let them be permanently removed.

How do I restore a receipt I deleted by mistake?

Go to Trash from the main menu. Any receipt or transaction you deleted will be there until it's permanently removed — tap Restore to bring it back.

How do I export my records for my accountant?

Go to the Export section, select your tax profile and year, and export to Excel — formatted to be German tax-ready.

How do I buy more scan credits?

Tap the Scan Receipt button on the home screen (or the scan tab). Once your scan balance reaches zero, this takes you straight to the purchase screen, where you can buy additional scan credit packs via the App Store.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Go to the purchase screen (tap Scan Receipt if your balance is zero, or open it from a low-balance prompt) and tap Restore Purchases. This will restore any scan credit purchases linked to your Apple ID.

Is my bank and receipt data secure?

Yes. Sensitive details like IBANs, account numbers, and card numbers are automatically masked before any AI processing. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I delete my account and all my data?

Email us at projects.madhavi@gmail.com with your account request, and we'll process the deletion of your account and associated data.

The app crashed or isn't working right — what should I do?

Please email us with a description of what happened, your device model, and iOS version, so we can investigate quickly.
